About Me

Anjan Lamichhane is an IT professional and digital entrepreneur with a multidisciplinary background in digital marketing, graphic design, photography, videography, video editing and professional training.

He has worked in IT professionally for 4+ years, in photography and videography for 5+ years, and as a trainer for 3+ years. Based in Nepal, he has also handled website, graphic design and video editing projects connected with the UK, USA and Dubai.

Anjan was born in Namjung, Gorkha. His family later came to Aanbukhaireni, Tanahun, and keeps homes in both places. His father runs a construction company, and his mother looks after accounts and finance.

His approach is simple: make technology easier and more useful for people and businesses.
